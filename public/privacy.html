Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Deadlock Labs ("we", "us") builds websites, web apps, mobile apps and AI tools for businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we look after it. We keep it short and plain.

Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, project type, budget range and project details.
  • Free website audit form: your website address, name, email and (optionally) your type of business.
  • Email and WhatsApp: anything you include when you contact us directly.

We do not ask for payment details on this website, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote or carry out your free website audit.
  • To communicate with you about a project you've asked us about or hired us for.
  • To keep basic records of client conversations and agreements.

We will never sell, rent or trade your information, and we won't add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Services that help us

Form messages may be delivered to our inbox by a form-delivery service (Web3Forms), and this site is hosted by a web hosting provider. These services process data only to provide their service to us. Our email is handled by our email provider.

Cookies and storage

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one small preference in your browser, the currency you choose on the pricing section (PKR or USD), so it's remembered next time. If we add privacy-friendly visitor analytics in future, we'll update this page.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and project records for as long as the working relationship continues and for a reasonable period afterwards. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Just email us and we'll handle it promptly.

Client projects

When we build a product for you, any data in it belongs to you. We access it only as needed to build, test and support the project, and we hand over all code, accounts and access when the project ends.
